About

I passed my baccalauréat in Morocco and moved to France at 17 to study aeronautics in Toulouse. Alongside my studies I worked at McDonald's, where I went from crew member to crew trainer.

In 2024 I joined ESIEE Paris on a work-study track with Airbus. I work in the CAMO, the team that manages the continuing airworthiness of aircraft during their projects. I am Fleet Manager there, in English, with colleagues in France, India and Malaysia.

I like making a complicated process simple to follow, and I am always looking for what I can do better. Abroad, I want to learn from a new team and bring what I know: process maps, tracking tools and dashboards.
